# The EM Pendulum 


Some time ago, my manager shared with me this (quite popular as it turns out!) blog post on switching between Engineering Management and Software Engineering roles. 

[The Engineer/Manager Pendulum](https://charity.wtf/2017/05/11/the-engineer-manager-pendulum/)

It’s seven years old now but has some really interesting, universal insights that helped me both in my career and in supporting others in theirs. 

Lately, Charity recorded a more comprehensive talk on the subject that goes into a bit more detail and context - 

[The Engineer/Manager Pendulum](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2BkdAgCYHdc)

TLDR; Some key insights:
- „The best technical leaders in the world are often the ones who do both. Back and forth.  Like a pendulum.”
- "**Management is not a promotion, management is a change of profession**.”
- „You’ll miss the dopamine hit of fixing something or solving something.  You’ll miss it desperately.” (if you move to an EM role) 
- “Hybrid manager+tech lead is an unstable combination because your engineering skills and context-sharpness are decaying the longer you do it.”
