# The Engineering Leader’s Checklist

The idea for this list evolved from simple notes I’ve been taking in preparation for job interviews.  I already had some experience (around 8 years) of being an Engineering Leader at various levels and industries, so I decided to write down key areas that are common to all of those and that you need to address at some point in any Engineering organisation. 
It helped me to prepare for interviews, to reflect on my own experience but also to avoid the stress-induced blackout in my head during an interview, so that afterwards I would not go „I knew this! Why didn’t I think of this?” - as it happened before in my past while interviewing… 
When I started to work on the list, adding items, and more details, I realized how helpful that would be to have when I was new to the leadership path of my career. 
So there you have it - this is not meant to be revolutionary or give any specific solutions to challenges you might be facing. It’s sort of a checklist of areas that you need to, as an Engineering Leader, consider and make sure they’re covered one way or the other. Some areas might overlap with other people from Product, Delivery etc. but it’s inevitable for you to be engaged in them, even if you won’t own them. 
Sprinkled with some (hopefully) useful resources :) 
Who this might be most useful for?
- People stepping into an Engineering Leader’s shoes
- People interviewing for an Engineering Leaser position 
- An Engineering Leader joining a new company
