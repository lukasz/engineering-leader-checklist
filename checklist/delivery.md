# Delivery 🚚

[The Engineering Leader’s Checklist](../README.md)

The purpose of this function is to effectively manage the work required to achieve the goals set in the roadmaps. While Roadmaps provide the content, Delivery would focus on the processes of bringing that content to life and maintaining it - in the most efficient way possible over both the short and long term. 

## Checklist
- **Discovery** - Making sure you have all your planned work informed - what are the functional and non-functional requirements, resources needed, dependencies etc. (e.g. Spikes, Design, incl. ADRs, PDRs etc.) 
- **Goals** - do you have clear, measurable and aligned goals that are clear for the team and help you plan meaningful work and minimise churn? 
- **Agile** - do you have a method to streamline your work flexibly - through scrum, kanban, or any other methodology that allows you to easily adjust course whenever you need. 
- **Resources** - do you have all the resources you need for the work to go smoothly - like designs, infrastructure, specs, etc? 
- **CI/CD**
- [Capacity Planning](delivery/capacity-planning.md)
- **On-call** - consider if you need 24/7 support for your system maintanance. 
- **Avoiding bottlenecks** - those can come from capacity, dependencies and knowledge silos (for example, a good principle to adhere to avoid or break knowledge silos is „at least two devs per initiative and no more than two initiatives per dev”)
- **[SDLC](delivery/sdlc.md)** - Software Development Life Cycle
- **Metrics for Delivery** - how do you measure the progress of work and its effectiveness?
