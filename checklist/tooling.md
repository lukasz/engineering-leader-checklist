# Tooling 🛠️

[The Engineering Leader’s Checklist](../README.md)

## Checklist
- **IDE** - support your developers with the right tools, this is their basic one! 
- **AI** (Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude etc.) - boost your team efforts with AI capabilities. 
- **Communication** - day-to-day comms, on top of email - instant messaging (like Slack), video calls (like Zoom, Teams etc.), 
- **Documentation** - like Notion, Confluence, Coda, etc. - usually chosen at the org level. 
- **Delivery Tracking** - like Monday, Jira, Asana, etc. - there are tons of options out there - this one will usually be decided across multiple departments/chapters of the organisation. In this case, in the context of engineering, it’s worth applying the criteria of choosing the right tool around integrations with other engineering tooling - like VCS or CI/CD, metrics tooling (like Sleuth, DX, etc.), design tools (like Figma) as well as supporting the processes designed for your team for delivery (Scrum, Kanban, SAFe, Lean, Crystal etc.)
- **Monitoring / Metrics** - e.g. Sleuth (for DORA), DX (includes DORA, SAFE and DevEx), Grafana (opensource dashboard tool), DebugBear, Sentry for observability, CloudWatch - and many more - this list could go on and on and will be specific to your team’s/company’s needs. However, a good set of tooling to monitor your crucial metrics (even custom-made tooling!) is extremely important to be able to identify opportunities, patterns, and issues - all in all, make good, informed decisions.
