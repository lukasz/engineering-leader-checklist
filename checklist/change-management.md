# Change Management ♻️

[The Engineering Leader’s Checklist](../README.md)

## Checklist
- **Urgency of Change** - make sure that the need for change is instilled in the team/organisation before you start actively acting on implementing the change itself.
- **Advocates & Detractors** - find allies who will help you advocate both the need for change and the change itself, and keep tabs on the ones who will oppose it so that you’re aware of their impact and can take their feedback in. 
- **Communicatio**n - overcommunicate at all stages - starting with the urgency of change, what and why needs to change, what the options are, what the plan is, how you’re going to measure it, how things are going, what challenges you encounter and how you tackle it, and last but not least - celebrate once it’s done! 
- **Rumor monitoring/management**  - be aware of what’s the talk around the water cooler - this will happen no matter what, so don’t fight it - manage it. Try to learn what it is, take in the feedback and address all the valid concerns in the ongoing communication. 
- **The Curve of Change** - there are five stages that people will go through throughout the process of change - they will manifest in different ways depending on the person, but as a bigger team, they will fall into those - make sure you understand them, where you are on the curve and what are the specific symptoms in your case - it will help to better manage comms and next steps.
