# 1:1s

[The Engineering Leader’s Checklist](../../README.md) -> [Pastoral Care 👐](../pastoral-care.md)

One-to-ones are primarily for the report. It’s their time to get whatever support they need from their manager to do their job the best they can and to grow in their roles. 

## Checklist
- **Prepare** - have a list of things you’d like to address - might be something related to HR processes, feedback, or addressing an issue individually (e.g. some conflict resolution related, etc.) 
- **Check-in** - Ask the open question to find out what’s the priority for them - e.g. „What’s top of mind?” 
- **Dig Deeper** - say „Tell me more” or ask „What else?” 
- **Go through your list**
- **How can you help?** - ask exactly that!
- **Summarise & take notes** - it’s important to have notes not just for yourself but something your report or even the potential new manager of the report to access. 
- **General Topics to cover** - you should make sure these are covered not necessarily in every meeting but overall, long-term:
  - Individual Development Plan
  - Performance 
  - Feedback 
  - Energy Management / Rest (e.g. look at their AL balance and make sure they use it, watch out for overwork and burnout etc.)
