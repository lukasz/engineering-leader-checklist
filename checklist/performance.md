# Performance 📈

[The Engineering Leader’s Checklist](../README.md)

In other words - Productivity. How to get the most out of your team to maximise the value they generate over time? 

## Checklist 
- **Motivation** - a crucial element to keeping any productivity above the surface! 
  - Key factors can be grouped as - autonomy, mastery & purpose [^1]
- **Metrics** - e.g. DORA, the Westrum Index, etc. 
- **Continuous Improvement** - iterative adjustment of your processes, tools and principles to help optimise productivity at any point in time - keep in mind that those will, in most cases, not be universal but depend on the team makeup & culture, size of the organisation, and the product itself. Good retrospectives are a very good tool to work on that. 
- **DevOps Culture**
- **DevEx \ DX** - the Developer Experience
