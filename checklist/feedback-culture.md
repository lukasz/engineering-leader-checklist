## Checklist
- **Retrospectives** 🤔 (move under delivery or culture or feedback?)
  - Tools
  - Process 
  - https://conversations.dora.dev/
  - Track and measure the outcomes! - review them in each retro
- **Track the Feedback** - e.g. [Feedback „Treasure Chest”](feedback-culture/feedback-treasure-chest.md) 
- **Traits of quality feedback**
  - Always back it up with examples
  - Focus on actions and their outcomes
  - Avoid general statement 
  - Don’t make it personal 
- Be proactive in promoting Feedback Culture
  - Give constructive Feedback whenever you can
  - Ask for Feedback regularly
