# R&D & Innovation 🔬

[The Engineering Leader’s Checklist](../README.md)

It is important to create space for innovation. Day-to-day, you mostly focus on the „one-to-10” work—optimising your existing product and growing numbers (customer base, market share, product metrics in general). But it’s easy to lose sight of the zero-to-one work, which is coming up with innovative new ideas that are crucial to keeping the competitive edge long term. 

### Checklist
- **Testing & Experimenting** (A/B tests, etc.) - make sure that experimenting and testing solutions are a part of your SDLC not just from a QA perspective but (maybe even more importantly) from an impact perspective - do A/B tests on new features and improvements to learn if they actually move the needle on your key product metrics. 
- **Ideas Hub** - create a space to gather ideas for innovation, experiments and all sorts of concepts that are not strictly a part of your recognised committed backlog - open it up to both top-down & bottom-up contributions, and provide support to evaluate the potential value and impact of the idea (some people have a great gut feel idea but lack the skill to frame them). 
- **“Hack days”** - this can take many forms - from full-blown hackathons to simply saving some capacity for people to work on innovation as part of their day-to-day. My favourite implementation of this is what I called „The Power Week” - 5 consecutive days of hyperfocus with no meetings whatsoever for 2-3 devs to build a PoC of an innovative idea (from the Ideas Hub) that ends in a demo and launching an experiment (most often in a form of an A/B test) - with all the needed resources (designs, research etc.) prepared in advance. 
- **Separate Invention and Optimization** - separate the zero-to-one work from one-to-ten work - the latter should be a part of your „regular” backlog, the zero-to-one ideas are your innovation.
