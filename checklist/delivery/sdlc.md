# SDLC

[The Engineering Leader’s Checklist](../../README.md) -> [Delivery 🚚](../delivery.md)

SDLC is a structured process the software development teams use to design, develop, and test high-quality software. It consists of several key phases:

- **Planning**
- **Analysis**
- **Design**
- **Development**
- **Testing**
- **Deployment**
- **Maintenance**

**Common SDLC models include**
* Waterfall (sequential)
* Agile (iterative)
* DevOps (continuous)
* Spiral (risk-driven)

The choice of SDLC model depends on project requirements, team structure, and organisational needs. Modern approaches often emphasise flexibility, continuous feedback, and frequent iterations.
