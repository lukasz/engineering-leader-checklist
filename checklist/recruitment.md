# Recruitment 🙋‍♂️

[The Engineering Leader’s Checklist](../README.md)

## Checklist
- **Process**
  - As short as possible
  - Look for culture fit and potential before hard skills (missing skills can often be thought)
  - Provide good feedback
- **Live Coding vs. Take Home Task**  - tread carefully when choosing between those two options. Both have their strengths and weaknesses. Live Coding might seem like a light touch and optimal solution (it makes the overall process shorter), but for many it’s very stressful and tests more the ability to work under pressure rather than coding skills, and you most likely won’t learn as much as you could about candidate’s capabilities. Take Home Task on the other hand does prolong the process, and creates a risk of the candidate opting out or deprioritising your process, but it’s much less stressful, you can evaluate the candidate’s skills more accurately and be more inclusive towards some very good candidates, who do not perform that well under that kind of pressure. Either way, keep in mind to:
  - **Make it as relevant to the job as possible**
  - **Give it a time cap** (eg. no longer than 1-2h to spend on it) - for both live and take-home tasks. 
  - **Consider an optimal combo** of time-capped Take Home Task to be done in between prescheduled interviews (reducing the risk of them backing out), with clear guidance, and setting the expectation of the follow-up interview to go through the solution - this way you combine the comfort, inclusivity and agility of the process. 
- [Engineering Recruitment Questions](recruitment/engineering-recruitment-questions.md)
- **Interviews** 
  - 2-way street - allow the candidate to learn about you as well 
  - Take notes and summarise right after the interview 
  - **Guiding principles**
    - Be cautious of biases!
    - Set diversity goals
    - Communicate the culture of your organisation
    - Try to make it as casual as possible
- **Areas/threads** - what you should try to learn about the candidate
  - Learning & Self-development 
  - Problem-solving & Product Engineering Mindset
  - Handling Feedback
  - Communication & Collaboration 
  - Role-relevant Hard Skills
