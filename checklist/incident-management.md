# Incident Management 🚨

[The Engineering Leader’s Checklist](../README.md)

## Checklist
- Clear channels
  - i.e., a dedicated channel for an incident 
  - Timely updates 
    - Any progress on resolving or investigating the issue
    - Relevant metrics/impact 
    - Dependencies?
- Comms owner - EM or a senior IC
- Post-mortem 
  - What has happened and why
  - How has it been resolved
  - What steps have been taken to avoid the situation in the future (or need to be - eg, what new processes/procedures need to be carried on)
  - Impact - what were the consequences of this incident?
