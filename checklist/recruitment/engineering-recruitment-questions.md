# Engineering Recruitment Questions

[The Engineering Leader’s Checklist](../../README.md) -> [Recruitment 🙋‍♂️](../recruitment.md)

### Questions 
- What are, in your opinion, the three most important traits of good clean code?
- Can you give an example of feedback or advice you were given that you have applied and it had a positive impact on you?
- Can you give an example of a situation when you had a difference of opinion with a colleague around a technical solution - what was it about and how it got resolved?
