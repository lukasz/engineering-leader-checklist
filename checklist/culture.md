# Culture 👐

[The Engineering Leader’s Checklist](../README.md)

## Checklist
- [Westrum Index](culture/westrum-index.md)
- Cultural differences/backgrounds
- Team Bonding, personal relationships 
- “The Culture Map” 
- Motivation
  - Autonomy, Mastery & Purpose [^1]
- The Boyscout Rule - „Leave the codebase cleaner than you found it”
- **Write down the principles** - not fluffy, specific and actionable (i.e. how easy it is to answer if you’re living up to it?)
  - Gather external examples
  - Reference some axiomatic well-known facts that were proven to have a positive impact (like DORA metrics - the counterintuitive fact that speeding up smartly is, in fact, making the software better -, [Westrum Index](culture/westrum-index.md), etc.)
  - Company / Engineering culture pitch - for recruitment, events etc.
