# Tech Radar 📡

[The Engineering Leader’s Checklist](../../README.md) -> [Technology 🦾](../technology.md)

This list is about getting a hold of what technologies you’re using and the state of those technologies (within their lifecycle). 

### Checklist
- **Regular Review Process** 
  - How do you introduce new tech?
  - Who makes the decision? 
  - What are the criteria for choosing technologies? 
  - How do you deprecate or completely move away from a technology?
- **Languages & Frameworks**
- **Infrastructure & Tools**
  - Databases 
  - CI/CD
  - Caching 
  - Iaas / Cloud
  - Asset Management 
- **Managing your Technology Lifecycle** - the actual Tech Radar tool is perfect for it, as it allows you to map all your technologies that are in four different categories - **adopt** (your go-to tech), **trial** (give it a proper test before adopting), **assess** (research before trailing), **hold** (getting rid of, deprecating).
