# Architecture 🏛️

[The Engineering Leader’s Checklist](../../README.md) -> [Technology 🦾](../technology.md)

This is the set of principles and blueprints - i.e. high-level design, of the entire system you’re building and maintaining. 

### Checklist
- **Diagrams** - good practice is to apply a good standard like UML or C4 
- **Review Forum** - important architectural decisions should not be made in solitude. No matter how big your team is - whether it’s a team of dedicated architects or some senior ICs - make sure you create a space for reviewing those decisions. 
- **Documentation** - you should have your Architecture documented - preferably beyond the diagrams, but also documents describing the diagrams in more detail and even how and why you decided for it to be that way (through ADRs for example)
- **Discovery** - have a dedicated time to properly research and analyse architectural decisions (ADRs or doing premortem analysis come in handy here as well) - make sure you allocate it regularly when you plan your work (e.g. using Spikes) 
- **System Design** - what to look for in a good system design:
  - Scaling policy
  - Data Management
  - Load Balancing
  - Backups
  - Architecture (DDD, Microservices etc)
  - Security
  - Data flow
  - Logging 
  - Caching
