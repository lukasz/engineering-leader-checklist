# Compliance 🧑‍⚖️

[The Engineering Leader’s Checklist](../README.md)

In some cases, most often depending on your industry, you’ll need to make sure that the software you’re building is compliant with specific laws, standards or policies. 

Checklist 
- **Legal** - like GDPR or its equivalents, specific market regulations, local labour law etc. 
- **Industry Standards** - e.g. ISO 27001 for security. 
- **Internal** - Company Policies and Regulations
